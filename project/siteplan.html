Investment Learning Hub

Site Name

Investment Learning Hub is a clear and welcoming name for a website about investment education. The word Hub suggests one place where beginners can find books, useful lessons, and financial news resources.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help beginners understand the basics of investing. It will introduce important financial concepts, recommend helpful investment books with short descriptions, and provide a link to reliable financial news. The content will focus on education rather than personalized investment advice.

Scenarios

  • What should I learn before buying my first stock?
  • Which investment books are suitable for beginners?
  • Where can I find trustworthy financial market news?

Planned Website Structure

The final website will have three original pages and one external navigation link:

Home

Introduce the website and guide visitors toward books and beginner lessons.

Books

Display real investment books with cover images, authors, and short summaries.

Knowledge

Explain basics such as stocks, bonds, and risk management, with a learning-preferences form.

News

A navigation link to an established financial news website, rather than a fourth page.

Planned interactivity: filter book cards with JavaScript and remember a visitor's selected learning interest using localStorage.
